## Provider evidence and limits

[Kraken OHLC](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-ohlc-data) defines the forming final bar, committed cursor and limited retained history. [Kraken historical OHLC guidance](https://support.kraken.com/ca/articles/360047124832-downloadable-historical-ohlcvt-open-high-low-close-volume-trades-data) describes intervals without trades. [Alpaca historical bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbarsingle-1) defines inclusive start/end timestamps and requires following pagination even on short pages; the adapter subtracts one millisecond for the contract's exclusive end. [Alpaca's FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq#how-are-bars-aggregated) explains why a clock interval can legitimately have no generated bar. Checked 2026-11-22 UTC.

Coverage means the provider's requested interval was queried under that API contract; it is not an independent reconstruction of every exchange trade. A provider that silently omits an interior bar while supplying consistent metadata cannot be distinguished from a genuine no-trade interval by this endpoint alone. Requiring a fixed count would invent a requirement the providers do not promise. Retained history without the known left anchor remains explicitly unavailable and retries; this change neither fabricates it nor introduces a new terminal halt/retry policy. The available endpoint cannot recover data beyond its retention.
