## First policy decision and remaining work

Trey answered the first question by favoring higher-timeframe evidence and asking for guidance: "i think that looking at higher timeframes would be a better indicator no? you are gunna have to help me on these questions". He also reiterated that he is learning both trading and coding. This is a preference and request for a recommendation, not an approved numerical scoring formula.

The recommended direction is to retain confidence as the first comparison, then use completed higher-timeframe evidence that supports each tied setup under its own strategy rules. Record support, conflict and unavailable evidence explicitly. Treating all strategies as trend followers, reusing a mixed lower/equal/higher-frame score as if it were higher-frame confirmation, or adding arbitrary confidence weights would change that meaning. For a 15m entry, 1h and 4h are higher-frame context; for a 4h entry, 1d is the only strictly higher frame in the approved seven-frame set. Which relevant frames and predicates each strategy uses still needs the producer map; this recommendation does not add a new gate or silently treat missing evidence as agreement.

Current source already captures bars ending no later than the decision candle and checks their account/broker/mode ownership (`modules/MultiTimeframeAdapter.js:270-286`). However, the central strategy-specific MTF cases cover EMA, MADynamicSR, RSI and OGZTPO and request fixed 1h/4h inputs (`core/StrategyOrchestrator.js:1346-1490`); the accessor at `:1016-1018` does not itself enforce that the requested frame is higher than the entry frame. The general MTF score blends all ready frames (`modules/MultiTimeframeAdapter.js:389-451`). Receiving all seven native frames and using the appropriate higher frames for a particular selection are distinct contracts.

For the explanation to Trey, the external references were primary sources: [TradingView's higher-timeframe data guide](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/faq/other-data-and-timeframes/) describes why developing higher-frame values can change and why completed values avoid that particular instability; [Fidelity's RSI guide](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/RSI) explains that oversold/overbought readings can persist in strong trends. Neither source proves that this proposed tie-break improves OGZPrime's profitability. The recommendation is an engineering interpretation of Trey's desired evidence-based, strategy-owned selection and must be verified on the bot's own behavior.
