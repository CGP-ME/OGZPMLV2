## LIQUIDITY READING (the foundation — never predict, let market communicate)
L1. A high RESPECTING previous highs (tap, hold, move away) = market
    telling you liquidity is building ABOVE it (stops + breakout orders
    stack). That high becomes a TARGET.
L2. A low that has SWEPT previous lows and moved away = liquidity SPENT —
    "a low I view NOT to have liquidity." Does not need to run again.
L3. LIQUIDITY BLOCK: a high/low that already took out a prior high/low —
    no reason for price to revisit → USE IT FOR STOP PLACEMENT (stop goes
    beyond it, small risk, defended by structure).

## THE MODEL (bullish; invert exactly for bearish)
D1. CONTEXT: price runs something from the left (old low taken) → bullish
    idea ACTIVATES. Identify the target: highs respecting highs (L1
    liquidity) above.
D2. ENGINEERED LIQUIDITY (the defining step — no EL, no model): on the
    way up, price prints a high that respects prior highs, then retraces
    = sellers deliberately entering = liquidity engineered above that
    high. This is the market's own confirmation the highs will be run.
D3. THE TRAP: price retraces; early buyers get induced at internal
    structure (high taken → low respecting low = retail buys stacking,
    liquidity building BELOW an identifiable low).
D4. ENTRY = the moment that low is SWEPT ("as soon as it gets stabbed").
    Do NOT over-refine (no hunting imbalances inside the sweep — missing
    entries costs more than precision buys). Buy the take-out of the
    trapped buyers.
D5. STOP: below the covering low from the left (a liquidity block, L3),
    with breathing room. TIGHT by construction — that's where the RR
    comes from.
D6. TARGET: the engineered-liquidity high from D2 / external highs.
    Structural target, market-communicated, NOT a fixed RR. Partials at
    internal ELs allowed but small (20-25% max) — "hold majority volume
    to the target; the analysis projected the level, take advantage of it."
D7. MANAGEMENT: when a high gets taken post-entry (buyers induced = move
    underway) → roll stop to break-even. Hold to target. Fixed daily
    max-risk figure governs everything.
D8. INVALIDATION: entry swept deeper ≠ bias wrong — the setup must
    REFORM (new induction, new trap) before re-entry. Bias intact while
    the model's high/low remain intact.
D9. FRACTAL STACKING: HTF Da Vinci = direction/bias; LTF Da Vinci (1m-5m)
    = entry into the HTF target. Stacked instances = the 1:10+ RR class.
    Claimed profile: high RR AND high win rate WHEN waiting for the
    high-probability instances — patience is the actual edge; the
    failure mode is trading every high/low instead of engineered ones.
