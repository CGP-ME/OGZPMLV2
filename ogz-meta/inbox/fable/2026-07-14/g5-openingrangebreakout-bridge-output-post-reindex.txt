### 1. THESIS → TRIGGER  

* **Opening‑range capture is overwritten** – `OpeningRangeBreakout._handleWaitingForOpen()` sets `this.openingRange` every time a candle falls inside the OR window, without checking whether an OR has already been recorded【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:164-170】. * **Breakout detection uses the possibly‑overwritten range** – the breakout test compares the current close to `this.openingRange.high/low`【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:188-199】. If the OR was overwritten, a breakout can be reported even though the price has not broken the *original* opening range.  

* **Thesis‑valid breakout can be missed** – because the OR may be overwritten by a later candle, the strategy may finish the OR window with a too‑narrow range and never see a genuine breakout that occurs after the window, so the trigger never fires.  

* **No guard for “first‑candle only”** – the code never verifies that the candle being processed is the *first* candle of the session (the comment says “first 15‑min candle”, but the logic does not enforce it).  

### 2. TRIGGER → DIRECTION  

* **Direction is set solely from the breakout test** – `this.breakoutDirection` is set to `'bullish'` if `close > OR high` or `'bearish'` if `close < OR low`【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:188-199】. This works for simple breakouts, but when the OR has been overwritten (see 1) the direction may be derived from an incorrect range, causing a *wrong‑side* signal.  

* **Counter‑example** – consider a session where the first candle (true OR) is 100/99, but a later candle within the OR window (price 101/100) overwrites the OR to 101/100. A subsequent candle closes at 102 (still above the overwritten high 101) → breakout is detected as bullish, while the true OR breakout would have been bearish (price fell below the original low 99).  

* **No additional guard** – the code never re‑evaluates the direction after FVG detection, so a mismatched FVG cannot correct a wrong direction.

**Verdict: incoherent** – the implementation does not reliably enforce the Opening‑Range‑Breakout + FVG thesis, mis‑assigns direction in edge cases, produces a confidence score that does not reflect thesis quality, and its exit contract is not tied to the required structural invalidation conditions. Moreover, the platform orchestration leaks state across symbols and can silently disable the strategy.

### 1. THESIS → TRIGGER  

* **Opening‑range capture is overwritten** – `OpeningRangeBreakout._handleWaitingForOpen()` sets `this.openingRange` every time a candle falls inside the OR window, without checking whether an OR has already been recorded【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:164-170】. A later candle in the same 15‑min window can replace the true first‑candle range, so the trigger may fire on a “breakout” that is actually just a price move inside the same window, violating the thesis.  

* **Breakout detection uses the possibly‑overwritten range** – the breakout test compares the current close to `this.openingRange.high/low`【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:188-199】. If the OR was overwritten, a breakout can be reported even though the price has not broken the *original* opening range.  

* **Thesis‑valid breakout can be missed** – because the OR may be overwritten by a later candle, the strategy may finish the OR window with a too‑narrow range and never see a genuine breakout that occurs after the window, so the trigger never fires.  

* **No guard for “first‑candle only”** – the code never verifies that the candle being processed is the *first* candle of the session (the comment says “first 15‑min candle”, but the logic does not enforce it).  

### 2. TRIGGER → DIRECTION  

* **Direction is set solely from the breakout test** – `this.breakoutDirection` is set to `'bullish'` if `close > OR high` or `'bearish'` if `close < OR low`【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:188-199】. This works for simple breakouts, but when the OR has been overwritten (see 1) the direction may be derived from an incorrect range, causing a *wrong‑side* signal.  

* **Counter‑example** – consider a session where the first candle (true OR) is 100/99, but a later candle within the OR window (price 101/100) overwrites the OR to 101/100. A subsequent candle closes at 102 (still above the overwritten high 101) → breakout is detected as bullish, while the true OR breakout would have been bearish (price fell below the original low 99).  

* **No additional guard** – the code never re‑evaluates the direction after FVG detection, so a mismatched FVG cannot correct a wrong direction.  

### 3. CONFIDENCE MATH  

* **Confidence ignores most thesis factors** – `_calculateConfidence()` starts at a flat 0.50 and only adds a fixed boost based on the FVG percentage size【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:307-317】. It does **not** consider opening‑range width, breakout strength, time‑to‑breakout, or any other quality metric.  

* **Potential inversion / dead‑zone** – any gap larger than 1.5 % receives *no* boost, leaving the confidence at the baseline 0.50 even though a larger gap could be a stronger signal. Conversely, a minimal gap of 0.3 % already receives a 0.15 boost, which may be over‑optimistic.  

* **Saturation** – the confidence is capped at 0.85, preventing any signal from ever reaching a higher confidence even if the configuration (e.g., `targetRR = 3`) would suggest a stronger conviction.  

### 4. EXIT FIT  

* **Exit geometry is generated from the FVG** – `calculateLevels()` builds `entry`, `stop` and `target` based on the FVG’s first‑candle high/low and the configured `stopBufferPct` / `targetRR`【modules/FairValueGapDetector.js:184‑226】.  

* **Config‑driven exit hints are overridden** – the orchestrator injects the strategy’s `exitContractHint` into the final signal【core/StrategyOrchestrator.js:2515‑2519】, but the strategy never checks the **structural invalidation conditions** listed in the config (`fvg_filled`, `or_break_reversal`)【config/trading.config.json:1349‑1352】. Consequently, an FVG that gets filled or an OR that reverses will *not* cancel the trade, violating the thesis that the exit must be tied to structural geometry.  

* **Missing validation of “max hold”** – the strategy’s `maxHoldTimeMinutes` (180) is only present in the exit hint【config/trading.config.json:1345‑1346】 and never enforced in the runtime logic.  

### 5. PLATFORM INTERACTION  

* **State is shared across symbols** – the orchestrator creates a single `OpeningRangeBreakout` instance (`this.orbStrategy`) at startup and then passes it to `_getSymbolStrategyModule()` for each symbol【core/StrategyOrchestrator.js:655‑657】. If `_getSymbolStrategyModule` returns the same instance for multiple symbols (the default behaviour when a cached instance exists), the internal state (`openingRange`, `breakoutDirection`, `recentCandles`) is **not isolated per symbol**【core/StrategyOrchestrator.js:1714‑1716】. This silently contradicts the strategy’s assumption of symbol‑scoped state.  

* **Toggle can silently disable the strategy** – the orchestrator’s `toggleMap` contains an entry for `'OpeningRangeBreakout'`【core/StrategyOrchestrator.js:1916】. If the pipeline flag `pipeline.enableOpeningRangeBreakout` is false, the strategy will be registered but never evaluated, yet the code still logs diagnostics and may produce stale `pendingSignal` objects.  

* **Session‑timing relies on DST‑aware `Intl`** – the ET‑based session detection uses `Intl.DateTimeFormat` to compute hour/minute【modules/OpeningRangeBreakout.js:149‑155】. If the host environment does not have the `America/New_York` timezone data, the hour calculation can be off by one hour, causing the OR window to start at the wrong time and breaking the thesis that the OR is the *first* 15‑min candle after session open.  

* **Min‑confidence gating is absent** – the config defines `minConfidence` (null)【config/trading.config.json:1347】, but the orchestrator never checks `signal.confidence` against a global minimum before emitting the trade. This allows a low‑quality 0.50‑confidence signal to pass through.

### Counter‑example where the breakout direction differs from the “true” opening range

| Timestamp (UTC) | Open | High | Low | Close |
|-----------------|------|------|-----|-------|
| 09:00 – 09:05   | 100  | 105  | 95  | 100   |
| 09:05 – 09:10   | 100  | 102  | 98  | 101   |
| 09:10 – 09:15   | 100  | 101  | 99  | 100   |
| 09:15 – 09:20   | 100  | 103  | 97  | 104   |

**Execution flow**

1. **09:00** candle satisfies the window condition → `openingRange` set to **high 105, low 95**.  
2. **09:05** candle also satisfies the condition → `openingRange` **overwrites** to **high 102, low 98**.  
3. **09:10** candle again satisfies → `openingRange` **overwrites** to **high 101, low 99**.

**Why this differs from the “true” opening range**

The implementation, however, replaces the range with later candles, causing a breakout that would not exist under the canonical definition.
