## Trey-approved deviation from original alarm semantics

“protected touch => red X + buzz” to health semantics: a protected touch emits
the max-priority buzz and remains GREEN because the alarm operated; RED is
reserved for failure of the alarm mechanism itself (range, Git, internal, or
HTTP failure). Legitimate protected-file missions are frequent. Making normal
detections red trains operators to ignore red and destroys the alarm. GREEN
means alarm healthy; when `NTFY_TOPIC` is absent it does not mean notification
delivery was verified. This deviation is recorded in this packet and does not
modify `TREY-RULINGS.md`.
